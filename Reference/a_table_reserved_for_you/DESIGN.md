## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, editorial hospitality experience centered around bespoke culinary journeys and sensory elegance. Rooted in tactile romanticism and quiet luxury, the UI evokes the physical presence of artisanal stationery: weighted deckle-edged linen stock, botanical dry-point etchings, delicate hairline gold foil, and the warmth of a candlelit dining room. 

The emotional tone balances poised anticipation with nostalgic intimacy. The interface shuns generic hospitality SaaS tropes, opting instead for deliberate pacing, editorial typesetting, and subtle dimensional references like pressed wax tokens, debossed borders, and linen-textured grounding surfaces.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from botanical flora, natural soils, and time-weathered textiles:

- **Primary (`#3A4A34` - Deep Forest Sage):** Evokes dense culinary gardens and velvet banquettes. Used for core brand moments, display headlines, structural framing, and high-emphasis controls.
- **Secondary (`#F5EFE3` - Warm Linen Cream):** Serves as the canvas foundation, carrying the subtle texture of unbleached tactile paper stock.
- **Tertiary (`#B5654A` - Burnt Terracotta):** Brings warmth and appetite appeal. Applied strictly for key calls to action, seasonal highlights, and celebratory badges.
- **Neutral (`#1F261C` - Deep Woodland Umber):** Replaces harsh jet black with an organic deep charcoal-olive for legible body copy and crisp structural dividers.
- **Romantic Accent (`#D9A5A0` - Dusty Rose):** Adds tenderness to course dividers, subtle hover fills, reservation status badges, and decorative botanical accents.
- **Detail Accent (`#C9A24B` - Muted Antique Gold):** Applied as refined hairline rules (0.5px–1px), seal accents, and foil flourishes.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial rhythm reminiscent of bespoke tasting menus and handwritten vintage invitations.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys literary grace and culinary heritage. Large titles rely on regular weights with negative tracking, while course titles and chef notes leverage standard italics to inject lyrical tenderness.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances romantic serifs with humanist clarity, geometric cleanliness, and legibility at petite sizes for course descriptions, ingredient pairings, and reservation calendars.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered in small-caps or uppercase with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.16em`), echoing classic print typography and stationery footers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout centers around generous breathing room, purposeful pacing, and framed composition.

- **Grid Model:** Desktop views use a centered, max-width fluid 12-column container (capped at 1200px) bounded by expansive canvas margins. Tablet uses an 8-column layout with 2rem margins, and mobile collapses to a single or 2-column stacked flow.
- **Atmospheric Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm mimics printed keepsakes. Tightly packed interfaces are strictly avoided; elements are given ample room to breathe, inviting deliberate reading.
- **Bilateral Framing:** Content blocks frequently sit within framed, double-bordered card enclosures to simulate menu inserts inside presentation folios.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and grounded, avoiding sterile, high-blur SaaS dropshadows.

- **Linen Surface Base:** The background utilizes layered ivory tones with subtle, semi-transparent noise overlays to simulate 300gsm cold-press cotton cardstock.
- **Foil & Inset Borders:** Elevation is established horizontally rather than vertically. Cards use 1px nested borders (`#C9A24B` at 40% opacity, inset by 4px from an outer border) rather than floating shadows.
- **Wax-Seal Accents:** Applied to confirmation seals, course milestones, and authenticated signatures. These elements feature dual-layer ambient shadows tinted with terracotta (`box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(181, 101, 74, 0.22), 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 38, 28, 0.12)`).
- **Pressed Insets:** Interactive input wells and toggle tracks employ a soft debossed effect using micro inner-shadows (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 38, 28, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The design language balances architectural discipline with soft organic curvature. A roundedness factor of `2` (8px base radius, 16px for card enclosures) evokes smooth, custom-die cut paper edges without straying into overly playful pill-heavy silhouettes. 

Wax stamp accents, calendar date badges, and sommelier pairing markers embrace circular `rounded-full` geometries to reference physical seals, signet stamps, and pressed botanical coin motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (The Invitation Seal):** Solid burnt terracotta (`#B5654A`) or deep sage (`#3A4A34`), text in warm cream (`#F5EFE3`). Finished with `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal padding, subtly rounded corners (`8px`), and an inner hairline gold inset on hover.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background with a crisp 1px gold hairline border (`#C9A24B`), text in neutral umber (`#1F261C`). Fills with muted dusty rose (`rgba(217, 165, 160, 0.15)`) on hover.
- **Ghost:** Text in primary sage or burnt terracotta, accompanied by an animated hairline underline that expands outward from center.

### Cards & Menu Containers
- **Tasting Menu Insert:** Structured card bathed in `#FAF6EE`, outlined by an outer 1px border (`#E4DAC9`) and a secondary inner gold hairline (`#C9A24B` at 35% opacity). Padded with `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Course Rows:** Horizontal flex rows separated by delicate botanical leaf dingbats or subtle dotted lines in antique gold. Course names rendered in serif; ingredients in low-contrast sans-serif.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Set against a crisp ivory field with an understated baseline or fully enclosed 1px border (`#D6CBB8`). When focused, transitions to deep sage (`#3A4A34`) with a soft terracotta glow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(181, 101, 74, 0.18)`).
- **Date & Party Selectors:** Styled as circular calendar dates within an earthy grid; selected states fill in deep sage with gold hairline rings.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Tasting Indicators (e.g., "Wine Pairing", "Pescatarian"):** Petite uppercase badges (`label-sm`), featuring a dusty rose wash background (`rgba(217, 165, 160, 0.2)`), terracotta text (`#B5654A`), and 0.5px borders in muted rose.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Custom Pickers:** Circular and soft-square indicators framed in deep sage. Checked states reveal a miniature botanical bud or golden checkmark icon, avoiding default system styles.

### Bespoke Elements
- **Botanical Dividers:** Centered horizontal ornaments utilizing delicate svg leaf fronds flanked by tapering hairline rules.
- **Wax-Seal Confirmation Stamp:** An embossed circular crest containing the restaurant's monogram, rendered in terracotta or antique gold, applied to finalize booking states.
